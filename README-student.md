# WebHaste Starter Site (Plain HTML/CSS)

## What to do first
1. Open `index.html` in Chrome.
2. Replace the TODO blanks with your own content.

NOTE: Navigation links are disabled in preview mode

## Rules (so your site works)
- Don't rename files unless you also update the navigation links.
- Keep `css/styles.css` in the `css` folder.
- Use real sources on the Sources page.

## Turn-in
Your instructor will tell you how to submit (usually a zip of your exported site).
